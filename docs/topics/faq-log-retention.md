# How long are activity logs kept?

<!-- type: faq -->
<!-- category: getting-started -->
<!-- tag: logs,retention -->
<!-- seo-title: %%post_title%% - %%sitetitle%% -->
<!-- seo-description: Learn how coreActivity's automatic and manual log cleanup affects retention. -->
<!-- seo-keywords: logs,retention,cleanup -->

The retention period is controlled in **Settings → Maintenance**. New installations default to automatic cleanup enabled with a period of 24 months, but your site's settings may differ. A scheduled daily job removes entries older than the configured period. Administrators can also delete selected entries or use the manual cleanup tool.

See [Log Data and Retention](log-data-and-retention.md) for details, including backups and the settings export.
